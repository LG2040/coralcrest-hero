How we work

The REEF Method

Generic delivery frameworks assume you control your critical path. Treasury and TMS projects don't: it runs through banks, SWIFT bureaus, custodians and regulators. REEF is built for that reality, and named for the reef-built limestone Bermuda stands on.

R

Reliance

Every external dependency is mapped before a date is committed. The plan is shaped around who you rely on, not the other way round.

E

Evidence

Progress is claimed only with proof: executed test cases, signed-off reconciliations, confirmed connectivity. No "90 percent done" without artefacts.

E

Execution by accretion

Small, permanent, compounding layers of working functionality. Each layer is load-bearing before the next is added.

F

Foundation transfer

The engagement ends with your team owning the structure. Documentation, runbooks and knowledge transfer are deliverables, not afterthoughts.

The REEF Method is proprietary to CoralCrest Consulting Limited.
